浪浪想找家 Support

浪浪想找家是一個查詢台灣收容動物認領養資訊的 iOS App，資料來自農業部資料開放平台。App 不需註冊或登入，也不需要任何權限即可使用全部功能。

浪浪想找家 (Stray Animals) is an iOS app for browsing adoptable shelter animals in Taiwan, using the official open data published by the Taiwan Ministry of Agriculture. No account, no sign-in, and no system permissions are required — every feature is available immediately after launch.

如何使用 / How to Use

  • 開啟 App 後會自動載入認養中的動物列表，往下捲動會自動載入更多。 Launch the app and the adoption list loads automatically; scroll down to load more.
  • 點選上方的分類（全部／狗／貓／其他／幼年／成年）可以篩選列表。 Tap a category chip (All / Dog / Cat / Other / Young / Adult) to filter the list.
  • 點選任一張卡片可查看動物詳細資料、收容所資訊與備註。 Tap any card to view the animal's details, shelter information, and remarks.
  • 在詳細頁可以開啟地圖查看收容所位置、撥打收容所電話，或分享這隻動物的資訊。 On the detail page you can open the shelter address in Apple Maps, call the shelter, or share the animal's information.

常見問題 / FAQ

  • 動物資料來自農業部資料開放平台，App 內資料可能因原始資料更新時間而延遲。 Data comes from the government Open Data platform and may lag behind the shelter's own records.
  • 動物照片可能因原始圖片連結或圖片服務狀態而暫時無法顯示，此時會顯示預設圖示。 A photo may be temporarily unavailable if the original image link is down; a placeholder icon is shown instead.
  • 列表無法載入時，請先確認網路連線，並稍後再試；原始開放資料平台偶爾會維護。 If the list fails to load, check your network connection and try again later — the government open data platform is occasionally under maintenance.
  • 若要確認認養狀態，請以收容所公告或電話聯絡結果為準。 To confirm whether an animal is still available, please contact the shelter directly.
  • 本 App 不會要求定位權限；開啟地圖只是把收容所地址交給 Apple Maps 顯示。 The app never asks for location permission; the map button simply passes the shelter address to Apple Maps.
